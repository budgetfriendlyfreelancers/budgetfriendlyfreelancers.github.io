About BFF Studio

We're a small collective of web professionals dedicated to building affordable, beautiful websites for small businesses, NGOs, and creatives.

Our story

We're not a big agency

BFF Studio is a collaborative team of designers and developers who believe great websites shouldn't require a fortune. We started with a simple mission: build fast, clean, mobile-ready sites for small teams, NGOs, artists, and startups — and hand over full ownership every single time.

We work directly with our clients, keeping costs low and quality high. No middlemen. No locked dashboards. Just honest work at honest prices.
